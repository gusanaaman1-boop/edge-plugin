EDGE - a two-sided musical filter
by Gussa Naaman


Draw the destination. EDGE travels there. Your sound pushes it.

  LOW and HIGH        where the two spectral edges are going
  EDGE                travels from CLEAN to CUT - one control, both sides
  FOLLOW to EDGE      lets the signal itself drive that travel
  SPREAD              moves the channels apart without narrowing either one
  BITE / WARM / IRON  hidden colour that arrives with the depth

At EDGE 0 it is a bit-exact wire and adds zero latency, so you can leave it on
a track and forget it is there.


WHERE THIS PUTS THINGS

  VST3 plug-in    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\EDGE.vst3
  Standalone      C:\Program Files\Naaman\EDGE\EDGE.exe

Close your DAW before continuing. Windows cannot replace a plug-in that a
running host has open, and that is the usual reason an update seems to do
nothing at all.

Afterwards, in Cubase: Studio menu, VST Plug-in Manager, Update. EDGE appears
under Naaman, in the Filter category.

To remove it later, use Windows' own Apps list - EDGE uninstalls like any
other program.


This build is not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen may show a blue "Windows
protected your PC" panel: click "More info", then "Run anyway". The manual,
MANUAL.md, is installed alongside the plug-in.
